PART 4 · TRUE OR FALSE

Listen and decide: true or false?

Teacher Listen to four statements. Statement one: A triangle with sides three, four and five is a right triangle. Statement two: The area of a circle is two pi r. Student Statement three: Parallel lines never meet. Statement four: The sum of the angles in a triangle is one hundred and eighty degrees.
1. A triangle with sides 3, 4 and 5 is a right triangle.

$3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25 = 5^2$, so it is a right triangle.

2. The area of a circle is two pi r.

The area is $\pi r^2$. $2\pi r$ is the circumference.

3. Parallel lines never meet.

In a plane, parallel lines stay the same distance apart and never meet.

4. The sum of the angles in a triangle is 180 degrees.

The three interior angles of any triangle add up to $180^\circ$.
